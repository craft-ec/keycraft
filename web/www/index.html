How identities work

An identity is a signing key and a store key, kept by a delegate that runs on your node; pages ask it to sign and never see the key. Every craftworks app on this node lists the same identities and lets you pick one, but only keycraft creates, imports, exports, renames or removes them. To use an identity on another device, Show keys here and import them there.
